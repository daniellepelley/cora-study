# Physics: AQA A level Physics (7408)

> Official spec, past papers and mark schemes: aqa.org.uk, "A-level Physics 7408". Check details against the current official specification.

## How it is examined (all at the end of Year 13)

| Paper | Content | Time | Marks | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | Sections 1–5 and 6.1 (periodic motion) | 2 h | 85 (60 written + 25 multiple choice) | 34% |
| Paper 2 | Sections 6.2 (thermal physics), 7 and 8, with 1–6.1 as assumed knowledge | 2 h | 85 (60 written + 25 multiple choice) | 34% |
| Paper 3 | Section A: practical skills and data analysis (45). Section B: one option topic (35) | 2 h | 80 | 32% |

- At least **40% of marks test maths skills**. Physics and maths support each other a lot here.
- The **Data and Formulae booklet** is provided. Cora must know which equations are *not* in it and practise picking the right one quickly.
- **12 required practicals** are assessed in the written papers. Practical competence is also assessed in school for the **Practical Endorsement** (CPAC), reported separately as Pass/Not classified.
- Command words matter: "state", "describe", "explain", "calculate", "show that", "estimate", "suggest". Know what each one demands.

## Content

1. **Measurements and their errors.** SI units, prefixes, uncertainties (absolute, percentage, combining), estimation.
2. **Particles and radiation.** Constituents of the atom, particles and antiparticles, the standard model (hadrons, leptons, quarks), conservation laws, the photoelectric effect, energy levels, wave–particle duality.
3. **Waves.** Progressive and stationary waves, polarisation, refraction (including total internal reflection and optical fibres), diffraction, interference, Young's double slits, diffraction gratings.
4. **Mechanics and materials.** Vectors, moments, motion (suvat, projectiles), Newton's laws, momentum, work, energy and power, density, Hooke's law, Young modulus.
5. **Electricity.** Current, p.d. and resistance, I–V characteristics, resistivity, superconductivity, series and parallel circuits, potential dividers, emf and internal resistance.
6. **Further mechanics and thermal physics.** Circular motion, simple harmonic motion, forced vibrations and resonance; thermal energy transfer, ideal gases, molecular kinetic theory.
7. **Fields and their consequences.** Gravitational fields and orbits, electric fields, capacitance (including charge/discharge), magnetic fields, electromagnetic induction, alternating current, transformers.
8. **Nuclear physics.** Rutherford scattering, radioactive decay, nuclear instability, nuclear radius, mass and energy, fission and fusion.

**Option (Paper 3B), one of:** 9 Astrophysics · 10 Medical physics · 11 Engineering physics · 12 Turning points in physics · 13 Electronics. _The school chooses. Confirm which, and record it in `profile.md`._

Sections 1–5 (and usually 6.1) are typically taught in Year 12 and 6–8 plus the option in Year 13. Check the school's order.
