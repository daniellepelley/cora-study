**Goal:** excel in Maths (Edexcel), Biology (OCR) and Physics (AQA). Final exams are **May–June 2028**.
**Written:** October 2026, early in Year 12. The `study-coach` agent keeps this up to date.

## The approach in one paragraph

Keep up with school week by week. Never let a topic slide past without understanding it. Use **retrieval practice and spaced review**, not re-reading. Put **exam technique** in early, so that by Year 13 past papers feel familiar. Use the strong overlap between Maths and Physics (and Maths and Biology statistics) to get double value. Protect sleep, rest and life outside school. Two years is a long run.

### Likely GCSE→A-level gaps to check early
- **Maths:** fast, accurate algebra (expanding, factorising, rearranging, fractions), indices and surds, completing the square, graph sketching.
- **Physics:** SI units and prefixes, standard form, rearranging equations, significant figures, basic trig for vectors. Also the idea of uncertainty, which is Section 1 and underpins practicals.
- **Biology:** cell structure, basic chemistry for biology (bonding, water, pH), the habit of precise vocabulary, and the step up from "describe" to "explain" answers.

## Weekly routine (draft, adjust in Phase 0)

Sixth forms usually expect roughly as many hours of independent study as lesson time. This routine is a starting point of about **4–5 hours per subject per week, including homework**. Adjust it to fit Cora's real week.

**For each subject, each week:**
1. **Homework** set by school. This always comes first.
2. **One tutor session (45–60 min).** Consolidate that week's lessons: explain it back, then exam-style questions, then mark them.
3. **Two short retrieval sessions (15–20 min).** Flashcards or quick-fire questions on *older* topics (spaced review).
4. **Tidy notes.** Keep a one-page summary per topic, written from memory then checked.

**Weekly:** a 20-minute check-in with `study-coach` (Sunday works well) to review the week and plan the next.

**At least one evening and part of the weekend completely off.**

## Using the overlaps

| Overlap | Maths (Edexcel) | Physics (AQA) | Biology (OCR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kinematics, forces, moments, projectiles | Mechanics 2–4 | Section 4 | – |
| Vectors | Pure 10 | Section 4 | – |
| Exponentials and logs, log graphs | Pure 6 | Decay, capacitors, required practicals | Population growth |
| Calculus | Pure 7–8 | Rates of change, SHM, area under graphs | Rates of reaction (gradients) |
| Statistics and hypothesis testing | Stats 1–5 | Uncertainties | Chi-squared, t-test, Spearman's |

When a topic comes up in one subject, the tutor should point out where it appears in the others.
